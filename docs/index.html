Things to try

    

      
Crash a leaderPress Kill on the broker that leads a partition. Its ZooKeeper session lingers for 6 seconds, sends to it retry, then the controller elects a new leader from the ISR.

      
Crash the controllerKill the broker marked Controller. When its session expires the others race to create /controller and one wins.

      
Stop cleanlyStop closes the ZooKeeper session at once, so failover is immediate instead of waiting for the timeout.

      
Lagging followerTick Pause fetching on a follower and keep sending. After 10 seconds without catching up the leader drops it from the ISR, then adds it back when you untick.

      
Lose two of threeStop two brokers. The survivor leads every partition with a one-broker ISR, and acks=all still succeeds.

      
Everyone downStop all three, then start one. Partitions whose ISR members are all down stay offline until one of them returns, so no committed message is lost.
